Getting Started

First Time Login

  1. Open smartpera.com/AdAstraCRM on your phone browser.
  2. Tap your name from the dropdown list.
  3. Enter your 4-digit PIN. Your manager Medz gave this to you.
  4. Tap Login. You're in.
📱
Add to your phone's home screen On iPhone: tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen". On Android: tap the menu (three dots) → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app". Now the CRM works like a real app. No more typing the URL.

Change Your PIN Right Away

Go to More → Change PIN in the bottom menu. Pick something only you know. This protects your client data.

🔒
Never share your PIN Your clients trust you with their personal information. If anyone else logs in as you, that trust is broken. Keep your PIN private.

Your Daily Morning Routine

☕
Open the CRM with your coffee. Every morning. 5 minutes. This single habit will change your results in 30 days. Promise.

The 5-Minute Routine

  1. Check 🎂 Today's Celebrations at the top. Send a birthday message to any client whose birthday is today. Takes 30 seconds.
  2. Look at 🎯 Today's Suggested Focus (Today's 5). These are the 5 leads the engine picked for you today based on urgency and potential.
  3. Tap WA or Email on each one and reach out. The message is already written for you — just review and send.
  4. Tap ✓ Done after you've worked each lead. The card disappears so you can see your progress.
  5. Check ⚡ Needs Attention for any leads that are hot, stale, or new and uncontacted. Reach out to anyone you missed.
🎉
When all 5 are done, you'll see a celebration screen "You're caught up for today!" That feeling never gets old. It means you did the work that drives results.
⏰
If you skip a day, the engine still works But you'll come back to a pile of overdue follow-ups and stale leads. Don't let it pile up. 5 minutes daily beats 1 hour weekly.

Adding a New Lead

How to Add

  1. Tap the big + button at the bottom right (or go to Leads tab → +).
  2. Fill in the lead's details. Don't worry — only Name is required.
  3. Tap Save Lead. The CRM remembers everything.

Which Fields Actually Matter

You don't need to fill in everything. Focus on the ones that help the engine work for you:

Always fill these in
  • Name — obviously
  • Phone — for WhatsApp button
  • Source — so the engine learns which sources work
  • Profession — helps target your content
  • Income range — affects priority score
  • Stage — defaults to New Lead
Skip these for now
  • Pass type if you don't know yet
  • Cases (you'll add later when proposing)
  • Follow-up date until first contact
  • FYC until you actually close
💡
Why source matters so much Right now your team's data shows TikTok converts at 60% and Referrals at 50%. If you don't tag sources properly, you can't see which sources actually make you money. Always pick the right source.

The Source Options Explained

Pick the one that best describes how you got this lead:

Referral — Someone you already know introduced you.
Client — An existing client who's bringing new business.
TikTok — They found you on your TikTok page.
Facebook — They found you on Facebook.
Social — Met at church, gathering, or social event (NOT social media).
Scorecard — They filled out the Financial Health Scorecard.
Lead Gen — From a calculator or quiz tool.
Website — From smartpera.com.
Advertisement — From a paid ad.
Event — From an event you attended (you'll add the event name).
Cold Activity — You reached out cold (street, cold DM, walk-in).
Others — Only when none of the above fits.

Lead Stages Explained

Stages are the heart of the CRM

Every lead is in one stage at a time. As you talk to them, you move them through stages. The engine watches these stages to decide who's hot and who's stuck.

New Lead
You just added them. You haven't talked yet.
→ Action: Reach out within 24 hours
Contacted
You sent them a message or made a call.
→ Action: Try to set a meeting
Follow Up
You scheduled a follow-up. They said "not now" or asked to talk later.
→ Action: Hit them up on the follow-up date
Met
You had a fact-find meeting with them.
→ Action: Send proposal within 48 hours
Proposed
You sent them a recommendation/quote.
→ Action: Follow up to close or handle objections
Closed
They signed up! Your client now.
→ Action: Enter FYC, set follow-up case opportunity
Rejected
They said no. Or you decided they're not a fit.
→ Action: Add reason. May still re-engage later.
⚠️
Always update the stage when something changes If you had a meeting but the lead still shows "Contacted," the engine won't know to push you toward sending a proposal. Stages = your reality. Keep them accurate.

Working a Lead

Opening a Lead

Tap any lead card. You'll see all their details — name, phone, email, source, history.

At the top, you'll see the 🧠 Smart Analysis panel. It's collapsed by default. Tap it to see what the engine thinks about this lead.

The Two Magic Buttons

📱 WA (WhatsApp) — Opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message based on the lead's stage. You can edit before sending. Always review first.

✉️ Email — Opens your Outlook (or default mail app) with a pre-written subject and body. Same thing — review and send.

✨
The messages are smart A "New Lead" gets a friendly introduction message. A "Proposed" lead gets a gentle check-in. A "Closed" client gets a relationship-building message. The engine writes different messages for different stages.

Updating Information

Tap Edit Lead at the bottom of the lead detail. Change anything — stage, FYC, remarks, next action, follow-up date.

The CRM saves changes automatically. The engine adapts the next time you open the dashboard.

Adding Activity Notes (Remarks)

After every conversation, add a quick remark. Example: "Called 3pm, said busy with kids. Try Saturday morning."

This builds the lead's history. When you come back in 2 weeks, you won't forget what they said.

🧠
Every note teaches the system The engine reads the date of your last contact. The more current your notes, the smarter your dashboard becomes.

Closing a Lead

When They Sign Up

  1. Open the lead.
  2. Tap Edit Lead.
  3. Change Stage to Closed.
  4. Enter the actual FYC in dollars. This is critical.
  5. Add the Case Types — Life Insurance, Critical Illness, Hospital, Investment, etc.
  6. Tap Save.
💸
If you don't enter FYC, your real performance is hidden A "Closed" lead with $0 FYC looks like you did nothing. The team's data shows most closed leads have $0 FYC entered. Don't be that person. Always enter the real number.

Set the Follow-Up Case Opportunity

After you mark a lead Closed, the CRM will ask: "Is there a Follow-Up Case opportunity later for this client?"

Examples:

• Closed Life Insurance only? → Future opportunity: Critical Illness rider
• Closed Hospital Plan only? → Future opportunity: Income protection
• Young client closed Education plan? → Future opportunity: Retirement
• Investment only? → Future opportunity: Protection foundation

Set the revisit date (default 90 days) and write a quick note. The CRM will remind you to come back to it.

💰
Follow-Up Cases = your hidden FYC pipeline Every closed client is a future case. The system tracks them, reminds you, and pushes you to mine your own client list before chasing new leads. Use it.

Understanding Smart Scores

What the Numbers Mean

Every active lead gets a priority score from 0 to 100. The higher the number, the more urgent.

The engine looks at: lead stage, days since last contact, follow-up date, FYC potential, follow-up case opportunities, and more. You don't need to memorize this. Just trust the score.

80+
🔴 URGENT
Drop everything. Contact today. Probably overdue or high-value at risk.
60-79
🟠 HIGH
Work this week. Strong opportunity or scheduled follow-up.
40-59
🟡 MEDIUM
Keep nurturing. Don't let it cool down.
0-39
🟢 LOW / MONITOR
No urgency right now. Will surface when something changes.
🎯
The lead list is auto-sorted by priority When you go to your Leads tab, the most urgent ones appear at the top. You don't need to search. The engine already did the work.

What the Smart Analysis Panel Tells You

When you tap to expand it, you'll see:

📊 What's Happening — Diagnosis of where this lead is right now
🎯 Suggested Next Action — What the engine thinks you should do
🩺 Coverage Insight — What insurance coverage gaps exist
🪪 Pass Context — S Pass vs E Pass vs PR considerations
⚠️ Flags & Hygiene — Things to be aware of (e.g. birthday coming, missing data)
💬 Suggested Message — Pre-written WhatsApp text you can copy

Birthdays & Anniversaries

The Easiest Win in Sales

Remembering a client's birthday costs you 30 seconds. The relationship payoff lasts years.

The CRM shows you today's birthdays at the top of your dashboard. Quick buttons let you send a WhatsApp or email greeting in seconds.

How to Capture Birthdays

  1. When adding or editing a lead, fill in the Date of Birth field.
  2. If they don't share it, tick "DOB not known".
  3. The engine will alert you 7 days before, then on the day itself.

Policy Anniversaries (For Closed Clients Only)

When you close a client, set their Policy Anniversary date. The CRM will remind you 14 days before the anniversary to schedule an annual review.

Annual reviews lead to riders, top-ups, and referrals. This is how you build your renewal income and follow-up case pipeline.

🎂
One birthday message can change everything Your client gets dozens of generic greetings on their birthday. Yours is from someone who actually helped them with their finances. They remember. That's how you become "their advisor."

Compliance Reminders

⚖️
You are a licensed representative of AIA Singapore Everything you send to clients — WhatsApp, email, social posts — must follow AIA's compliance rules. This is not optional.
Always do
  • Review every CRM-suggested message before sending
  • Use neutral language ("planning," "options," "review")
  • Identify yourself as an AIA representative
  • Keep client information confidential
  • Document conversations in remarks
Never do
  • Name specific AIA products in cold outreach
  • Give specific advice without a fact-find
  • Promise returns or guarantees
  • Share screenshots of client data
  • Forward messages with policy details to others

The Pre-Written Messages are Safe

The CRM's WA and email templates are designed to be compliance-safe. They don't name products, don't promise returns, and use neutral language.

If you edit them — that's on you. Stay neutral. When in doubt, ask Medz before sending.

Common Questions

My leads aren't showing up. What do I do?
Pull down at the top of the Leads list to refresh from the cloud. If still missing, check that you're logged in as the right user (top right). If still missing, message Medz.
Why is one lead at the top with a red URGENT badge?
The engine sees something urgent — usually an overdue follow-up, a hot lead going cold, or a Met-stage lead that needs a proposal. Tap it and the Smart Analysis panel will tell you exactly what.
Can I see other agents' leads?
No. You only see your own leads. Only your manager has the master view to see all agents. Your data is private.
What if I lose my phone? Will I lose my leads?
No. Everything syncs to the cloud automatically. Log in from any phone or computer with your PIN and your data comes back.
I added a lead but it's not in Today's 5. Why?
Today's 5 picks the top 5 most urgent leads. A brand-new lead has lower urgency than an overdue follow-up. After you mark it "Contacted" and a few days pass, its priority will rise.
The engine suggested a message, but it doesn't fit my client's situation. Can I change it?
Yes — always. The pre-written message is a starting point, not a script. Edit it to match the conversation. Just keep it compliance-safe.
I haven't used the CRM in a week. Where do I start?
Open it. Don't feel guilty. Just look at ⚡ Needs Attention and your Today's 5. Work the top 3. Move forward. Don't try to "catch up everything" — that's overwhelming. Just start again.
Why does the engine show different leads each day?
Because time changes everything. A lead you contacted yesterday is fresh. A lead you contacted 14 days ago is going stale. The engine recalculates priority every time you open the app. That's the magic.
